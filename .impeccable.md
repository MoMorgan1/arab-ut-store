## Design Context

### Users

Arab UT serves Arabic-first FC 27 customers, with complete English support for customers worldwide. Most customers arrive on mobile and want to configure and purchase Coins or another FC service quickly, with clear platform, delivery, quantity, and price choices. Arabic is the default experience and must be designed natively for RTL rather than treated as a translation of the English layout.

### Brand Personality

Confident, premium, and direct. The experience should feel like a trusted specialist FC service desk: energetic enough for gaming customers, but controlled and credible around account details, pricing, payment, and fulfillment.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the established Arab UT visual language: the existing logo, near-black and deep-navy surfaces, warm gold accents, stadium atmosphere, compact gaming-service cards, and Thmanyah Arabic typography. What is already built in this repository is the primary visual reference. New work should refine spacing, hierarchy, accessibility, and responsiveness without turning the brand into a generic SaaS dashboard. Surfaces and fields use the warm-black glass treatment the Coins configurator established (translucent warm-black tint, soft blur, a thin gold border and a top highlight; owner decision 2026-09-01), never white-frosted or blue glass. Avoid cyan or purple neon, generic platform logos when branded artwork exists, repeated nested cards, and other obvious AI-generated design patterns.

### Design Principles

1. Start from the components this repository already ships. Their structure, interactions, official assets, Thmanyah typography, warm black/gold visual language, and copy intent are the primary UI source of truth, and new work extends that vocabulary rather than inventing a second one.
2. Keep the primary purchase path obvious and compact, revealing only the next meaningful decision.
3. Treat Arabic RTL and English LTR as equal, intentional layouts with the exact public brand label `Arab UT` in both.
4. Reflect the actual business model in the interface: Coins use one combined `PS / Xbox` console option and one `PC` option.
5. Use gold for decisions and progress, not decoration; reserve visual emphasis for the current choice, current price, and next action.
6. Build mobile-first, keyboard-accessible, reduced-motion friendly interactions with clear loading, unavailable, validation, and success states.
7. Call Coins `كوينز` throughout Arabic customer-facing UI and use a light, widely understood Gulf tone rather than formal system language or heavy slang.
8. Use `frontend-design` and the relevant Impeccable skills to improve hierarchy, spacing, accessibility, responsiveness, and interaction quality without replacing the established Arab UT identity.

### Required UI Workflow

1. Inspect the relevant code already in this repository and the patterns it established.
2. For a new or redesigned interface, put the proposed screens on a `/design` canvas first and get Mohamed's approval there.
3. Load `frontend-design`, then load the relevant Impeccable skills. Use `polish` as the final UI pass.
4. Reuse an existing pattern before introducing a new one; a second way to do the same thing is a cost.
5. Treat generic design-system output as advisory. Reject any generated palette, font, layout, or effect that conflicts with the established Arab UT identity.
6. Ask Mohamed before any consequential departure from what the storefront already does.
7. Verify Arabic RTL and English LTR at 320px, 390px, 768px, and 1440px, including keyboard focus, 44px touch targets, reduced motion, overflow, and browser console health.
