## Design Context

### Users

Arab UT serves Arabic-first FC 27 customers, with complete English support for customers worldwide. Most customers arrive on mobile and want to configure and purchase Coins or another FC service quickly, with clear platform, delivery, quantity, and price choices. Arabic is the default experience and must be designed natively for RTL rather than treated as a translation of the English layout.

### Brand Personality

Confident, premium, and direct. The experience should feel like a trusted specialist FC service desk: energetic enough for gaming customers, but controlled and credible around account details, pricing, payment, and fulfillment.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve recognizable continuity with the current Arab UT WordPress storefront: the existing logo, near-black and deep-navy surfaces, warm gold accents, stadium atmosphere, compact gaming-service cards, and Thmanyah Arabic typography. The current live storefront and the approved assets in the WordPress export are the primary visual references. The replacement should refine spacing, hierarchy, accessibility, and responsiveness without turning the brand into a generic SaaS dashboard. Avoid cyan or purple neon, decorative glassmorphism, generic platform logos when branded artwork exists, repeated nested cards, and other obvious AI-generated design patterns.

### Design Principles

1. Start from the real Arab UT identity and assets; redesign for quality without erasing what customers recognize.
2. Keep the primary purchase path obvious and compact, revealing only the next meaningful decision.
3. Treat Arabic RTL and English LTR as equal, intentional layouts with the exact public brand label `Arab UT` in both.
4. Reflect the actual business model in the interface: Coins use one combined `PS / Xbox` console option and one `PC` option.
5. Use gold for decisions and progress, not decoration; reserve visual emphasis for the current choice, current price, and next action.
6. Build mobile-first, keyboard-accessible, reduced-motion friendly interactions with clear loading, unavailable, validation, and success states.
7. Call Coins `كوينز` throughout Arabic customer-facing UI and use a light, widely understood Gulf tone rather than formal system language or heavy slang.
