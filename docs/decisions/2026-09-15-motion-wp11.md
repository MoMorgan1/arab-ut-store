# Motion WP11 — the long tail

**Date:** 2026-09-15. **Decided by:** Mohamed (picker, full scope). Follows
[2026-09-14-motion-system.md](2026-09-14-motion-system.md), which left WP11
unscoped until the primitives existed.

## Scope

Of the 63 customer-facing candidates outside the seven primitive patterns in
`docs/motion-findings.md`, thirteen sites stay, in three small primitives:

1. **Disclosure** — a block opens and closes in place (grid row 0fr↔1fr, or a
   fade-and-settle for an anchored popover), stays mounted after its first
   open, and is `inert` while closed. Sites: FAQ answer (`::details-content`,
   pure CSS, older browsers keep the snap), account order-row details, cart
   coupon field, Rivals PC launcher block, store preferences panel.
2. **Fill** — a bar grows to its value on mount: loyalty progress, reviews
   rating distribution, service-slider track.
3. **Appear** — a badge that arrives after an edit settles in (profile
   "unverified" after a save; one present on load stays still).

Purchase-path sites (coupon field, slider track, reviews bars) go through a
`/design` canvas first, as the motion decision requires. The rest is
refinement and ships without one.

## Cut, and why

- Admin-shared `ui/*` primitives (alert, badge, checkbox, dropdown, table,
  spinner): admin motion is out of the plan.
- Login remember-me checkbox: removed in #151.
- Tabs-sliding highlights (login method, bottom nav, category filters, SBC
  platform): a highlight that slides between controls contradicts the glass
  language, where every control draws its own edge.
- Texts-reveal on static pages (footer, information page, sitemap, alerts):
  present on load; the scroll reveal from #95 already covers entrances.
- Pending-button shimmer and "thinking" states: the spinner pattern from #157
  is the store's waiting cue.
- Cart-added-notice stacking and resize: the mini-cart sheet is the design.
- Card tilt, login panel slide, input-clear dissolve, native phone `<select>`:
  decoration or browser-owned.
- Link underline hovers: the audit named `ui/button`'s link variant (admin)
  and two pages whose links carry no store styling; nothing to ease.
