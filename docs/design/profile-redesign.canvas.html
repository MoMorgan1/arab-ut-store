Home Orders Wallet Account
  • Warm, restrained filter — blur 24px, saturate 140%, brightness 0.86 (today: 34px, 190%, no brightness). Apple's regular variant adjusts luminosity rather than lifting it, and warmth is meant to come from the tint, never the filter.
  • Scroll edge effect — a mask-image gradient dissolves content into the bar's top edge instead of meeting a hard line. This is the cheap stand-in for Apple's lensing, and it is what stops the bar reading as a generic glassmorphism rectangle.
  • Two-hairline specular rim — gold top hairline brighter than the bottom one, so the bar reads as a convex slab. The large diffuse drop shadow is gone, replaced by a tight 0 -8px 24px.
  • Concentric geometry — the inner capsule radius is calc(surface − inset), Apple's concentricity rule, with the tokens named so a later corner-shape: squircle is one line.
  • Touch reaction on the compositor — :active scales to 0.96 and drops to 92% opacity; the sheen moves by opacity alone. No pointermove handler writing paint properties.
  • Four-way accessibility net — opaque #0E0A08 when backdrop-filter is unsupported, when prefers-contrast: more, and when prefers-reduced-transparency; 0.01ms transitions under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Deliberately skipped — SVG feDisplacementMap refraction: Chromium-only, silent in Safari, and WebKit's own test case currently crash-loops the GPU process. Also skipped: chromatic aberration and JS pointer tracking. Sources: docs/research/liquid-glass-bottom-nav.md.
  • Overflow — four items share the width (flex: 1 1 0), so no horizontal scrolling and no clipped fifth item.
